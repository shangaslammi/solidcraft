# Clipped-sphere bore after removal of the proto-topology status flags

Main now propagates failures when it combines or removes temporary vertices. The merge preserves that behavior. It keeps vertices separate when the periodic trim and its fixed vertex are both provably outside the opposite face’s tolerance. Main also moved shared-edge classification into its overlap-group replacement function. The PR’s body and edge tolerances now apply there. If obtaining a curve’s bounds or solving for coincidence fails, the caller receives the cause. The bore opened on both sides of the rim at offsets of 0.0000125, 0.000015 and 0.00002 mm. With both model tolerances increased tenfold, the bore also opened at the tolerance boundary and at ±1.25, ±1.5 and ±2 times that tolerance. The saved subtraction replayed with zero relative volume deviation. The 22.75 mm rounded outset was refused with `OverlapGroupNotReplaced` and `find_interval` as its cause, as main now requires. The vertex-removal union, both carried-body cuts and the drilled-frustum shell regression passed. All 45,692 cases across the 19 affected golden units matched, as did the checks for both workspaces and the kernel WebAssembly build.
